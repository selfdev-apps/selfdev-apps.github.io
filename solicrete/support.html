How the numbers work

Volume = length × width × thickness (or the shape's own formula), plus the extra you choose for waste. Bag counts use typical premixed yields: 80 lb ≈ 0.60 ft³, 60 lb ≈ 0.45 ft³, 40 lb ≈ 0.30 ft³, always rounded up. Ready-mix is rounded up to the quarter yard.
