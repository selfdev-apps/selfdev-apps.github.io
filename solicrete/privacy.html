Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

We collect nothing

Solicrete has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Your projects stay on your iPhone

Saved projects are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Voice input

If you use "Just say it", your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, on your iPhone when it supports it, otherwise by Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. Solicrete only reads the sizes from that text and keeps nothing.

Purchases

Solicrete Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Children

Solicrete collects no data from anyone, including children.
